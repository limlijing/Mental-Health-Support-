# Mental Health APP

 In recent years, the shift toward remote work and a digitally-driven lifestyle has
 quietly reshaped human connection. Many people now live behind screens, rarely
 finding the space to speak their hearts, and often carrying their worries alone without
 knowing how to tend to their inner storms. Although conversations about mental
 health have become more visible, true support remains out of reach for many because
 limited by cost, time, stigma, or simple exhaustion. In this widening gap between
 need and access, the demand for accessible, compassionate digital mental-health tools
 continues to rise.
 Many individuals still struggle to maintain emotional well-being because they
 lack timely support, hesitate to seek professional help, and long for a private,
 judgment-free place to express their feelings. Without simple tools to manage stress in
 their daily lives, emotional burdens quietly accumulate. Traditional therapy remains
 expensive or inaccessible, and not everyone feels safe asking for help face-to-face.
 Therefore, there is a clear need for a user-friendly digital platform that offers
 immediate, affordable, and non-judgmental mental-health support to help people
 steady themselves even when life feels overwhelming.
 In this project, we aim to create a mobile application that provides daily mental
 health support. Our app includes an Al chatbox that offers empathetic responses and
 self-care suggestions, a stress assessment survey to help users understand their
 emotional state and a daily journal section where users can freely express and reflect
 on their feelings. We also create two mini games like Bubble Pop game and Snake
 Game in our app. So, the users can relieve their stress through playing games.
 Together, these features encourage self-awareness and emotional balance in a simple
 and accessible way.
